Tech Hero

Kimberly Bryant

  • Engineer
  • Black Girls Code founder
  • Advocate for Equity in Tech

Kimberly Bryant is an engineer and the founder of Black Girls Code, a nonproft organization created to expand access to technology education for black girls and other underrepresented youth. Bryant noticed that the lack of diversity in technology was not simply about interest or ability: access, representation, mentorship, and a sense of belonging shaped who feels that they have a place in the tech industry. Instead of only pointing out the problem, Bryant helped create a space where young people could learn coding, computer science, and other technical skills while looking up to creators and leaders with a diverse background.

Bryant’s work challenges the idea that increasing diversity means simply bringing more people into the room. She asks who gets access to knowledge, opportunities, networks, and power. Black Girls Code reports that it has reached more than 53,000 learners through live and virtual workshops since 2011.
